About The Vibe Check Project

A free tool for sending the right words to the right person, at exactly the right time.

no account,
nothing to install

Most of us have been there. A friend goes quiet, someone you love is going through a breakup, a coworker just got some hard news. You want to say something real, but the cursor just blinks at you, and you end up sending nothing.

The Vibe Check Project was built for that exact moment: pick a card that says what you're feeling, add a short note, and send it. Every day there is also one kind word waiting for you on the homepage.

How it works

Three steps, and the third is theirs.

  1. 1

    Pick a card and the words

    Choose a design, then choose the words that fit, from general encouragement to sets written for specific situations.

  2. 2

    Add their name and a note

    Sign it or leave it anonymous. If it is for a particular day, seal it until that date and it stays closed until then.

  3. 3

    Share the link

    You get a link to text or email. They open it in a browser, on any device, with no app and no account. Every card ends with a way to send one back.

Privacy

What happens to your words.

  • The card travels inside its link

    Cards are not stored on a server. The names, the words and your note are packed into the link you share, so the card exists wherever that link goes, and anyone who has the link can open it.

  • Your history stays in this browser

    My Vibes (the cards you have sent), the days you kept and your streak are saved in this browser only. They don't follow you to another device, and clearing your browser data removes them.

  • Two optional fields do send something

    If you type their email address, the card link is emailed to them through EmailJS, with the names you entered. If you ask for the 30-day reminder or the morning email, your email address goes to MailerLite, which sends those emails.

  • What gets counted

    Simple counters note that a card was sent or opened (that is how My Vibes can show a card as opened), never what it said. Pages also load Microsoft Clarity, which shows how the site is used. Premium is paid through Stripe; your card number never reaches us.

We never sell your data, and there are no ads. Read the privacy policy
