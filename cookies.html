1. What Are Cookies & Web Technologies?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. In addition to HTTP cookies, modern web applications utilize client-side Web Storage (localStorage), web beacons, and pixels to deliver interactive functionality and evaluate performance.

This policy provides an honest, transparent breakdown of every category of cookie and local storage technology deployed on The Vibe Check Project (thevibecheckproject.com).

3. Client Storage (localStorage) vs. Traditional Cookies

We prioritize browser localStorage over server-side session cookies. Local storage data resides entirely on your device and is not automatically transmitted with every network request to our servers.

You may view or clear your local storage at any time:

  • In Chrome/Edge: Press F12 → Navigate to Application → Local Storage → Click Clear All.
  • In Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data → Remove.
  • In Firefox: Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data.

4. Browser-Level Cookie Management

Most web browsers allow you to manage or block cookies through their global settings. Disabling cookies will not affect the core free card-sending features. Clearing local storage removes your My Vibes history and your Premium unlock on that browser; email us with your Stripe receipt and we will restore Premium.
