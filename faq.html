Sending cards

Yes, 100% free forever. Anyone can pick a theme, write a personal message, and generate a shareable card link without paying or entering a credit card.

Never. The recipient simply clicks your private link and their personalized 3D card opens immediately in their mobile or desktop browser, accompanied by ambient soundscapes and animations.

When you add your note you can pick a date under "Seal it until a day". You still share the link right away. The card then stays closed on the recipient's device until that day on their own calendar. Until then it shows the date and offers to add it to their calendar. Sealing a card is free.

It is a courtesy seal, not a lock: the words travel inside the link itself, and nothing is emailed on the day. Your own preview is never sealed.

The optional $4.99 one-time unlock unlocks writing your own affirmation, the Calm, Celebrate, Love and Healing collections, 17 premium backgrounds (6 of them animated) and 5 extra sounds. It is saved in the browser you buy it on. It also helps cover our server costs to keep the platform free for everyone else. If you clear your browser data, switch devices or have any problem with a purchase, email WeCare@TheVibeCheckProject.com and we'll sort it out.

Today's card and the daily email

It is one sealed card on the homepage, new each day. One tap breaks the seal and turns it over. The words are the same as that morning's email, so the site and your inbox match.

Once it is open you can send it to someone, save it as an image, keep it, copy it, or take three slow breaths with it.

Yes. Open today's card on the homepage, choose "Save image", then pick "Phone wallpaper" (1179 by 2556 pixels) or "Square, for posting" (1080 by 1080). The image is made in your browser. On phones that support it, the share sheet opens so you can save it to your photos.

Days you have kept can be saved as a wallpaper again later from My Vibes.

Every morning, a fresh, thoughtfully crafted affirmation lands in your inbox. No sales pitches, no spam, just a grounding breath to anchor your day. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom.

Privacy and what is stored

Absolutely. You can sign your card with your real name, a nickname, or leave it signed as 'A friend', 'Someone who cares', or completely blank.

Yes. Visit your My Vibes Dashboard to see past cards created on this device, copy their share links again, check if they've been opened, or delete them. Everything is stored locally in your browser with zero logins required.

In this browser only. The days you opened, your streak and the days you kept are saved in this browser's own storage and are never sent anywhere.

That also means they do not follow you to another device or browser, and clearing your browser data removes them. Your kept days are in My Vibes.
