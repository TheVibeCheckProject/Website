Privacy Policy

Transparency by Design • Stateless Sharing, Telemetry, and Your Global Privacy Rights

📅 Effective Date: September 20, 2026
🛡️ Standards: GDPR (EU/UK), CCPA/CPRA (CA), COPPA, CalOPPA
📬 Privacy Contact: WeCare@TheVibeCheckProject.com

1. Introduction & Operational Philosophy

The Vibe Check Project ("we," "us," or "our"), founded and maintained by Devin Griffin, operates thevibecheckproject.com (the "Platform"). We believe privacy is an intrinsic human right, especially when seeking emotional support and positive encouragement.

Our core technical architecture is engineered around client-side statelessness. We do not require user account registration, password creation, or cloud profiles to create or receive affirmation cards. This Privacy Policy details the exact types of data we collect, why we collect it, how it is processed by vetted third-party vendors, and your rights under global privacy laws.

2. Technical Architecture: How Your Cards Work

🔒 ZERO SERVER STORAGE FOR CARD MESSAGES

Unlike traditional social media or messaging platforms, we do not store your personalized card messages, sender names, or recipient names on any central server or database.

When you compose a card, the text is compressed and encoded entirely within your browser into a Base64URL query string (e.g., view-card.html?data=...). When your recipient opens the link, their web browser decodes the data locally on their screen. The message exists solely in the URL link you share and in your device's local memory.

3. Information We Collect & How We Collect It

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Email Subscriptions (Daily Spark Newsletter): If you choose to subscribe to our free daily affirmation list, we collect your email address. Processing is handled via MailerLite. You may unsubscribe with a single click at any time.
  • Direct Inquiries: If you contact us via email at WeCare@TheVibeCheckProject.com or through our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and correspondence text solely to answer your inquiry.

B. Information Stored Locally on Your Device (Client Storage)

The Platform uses standard browser localStorage to store preferences locally on your physical device. This data never leaves your browser:

  • vibe_saved_cards: Stores a local history of cards you have created so you can view them on the "My Vibes" page;
  • vibe_theme_concept: Remembers your UI design preferences (Editorial vs. Playful Kinetic);
  • vibe_premium_unlocked: Stores local verification of your one-time Stripe Premium Unlock.

You can inspect, export, or wipe this data at any time through your browser developer tools or settings.

C. Automated Telemetry & Analytics (Microsoft Clarity)

To understand how visitors navigate the Platform and identify broken UI elements, we utilize Microsoft Clarity. Clarity captures behavioral metrics, heatmaps, and session replay.

  • Clarity logs browser type, operating system, approximate geographic region (country/state level), time on page, and scroll depth.
  • Clarity operates with automated keystroke masking, ensuring any text typed into input forms is obscured and never recorded.
  • For details, review the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

D. Monetization, Display Ads & Third-Party Cookies (Google AdSense)

📢 THIRD-PARTY DISPLAY ADVERTISING

We use Google AdSense (Publisher ID: pub-2492108586092522) to display web advertisements on eligible content pages (such as advice guides, situation directories, and message collections) to help fund free hosting and card delivery.

Ad-Free Safe Zones: In keeping with our wellness mission, ads are strictly prohibited from personal card unboxing screens (view-card.html), the card creation canvas (send-card.html), private card history (my-cards.html), and emergency crisis support sections (#crisis).

Google and third-party advertising vendors use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) and web beacons to serve interest-based advertisements based on your prior visits to our Platform and other websites across the Internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.

Managing Personalized & Interest-Based Advertising:

  • You can opt out of personalized Google advertising and manage ad preferences at any time by visiting Google Ad Settings and Google My Ad Center.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for interest-based advertising via the Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA / AboutAds.info) or the European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA).
  • For full details on Google's advertising practices and data privacy policies, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

E. Commercial Payment Processing (Stripe & Ko-fi)

When purchasing the $4.99 Premium Unlock or offering a tip:

  • Stripe, Inc.: Processes credit and debit card transactions for Premium Unlocks. Payment credential entry occurs inside encrypted Stripe elements. We never see, process, or store raw credit card numbers or CVV codes. See Stripe Privacy Policy.
  • Ko-fi: Processes voluntary tips and donations. See Ko-fi Privacy Policy.

F. Affiliate Links (Amazon Associates)

Our blog features reading recommendations with Amazon affiliate links. If you click on an outbound Amazon link, Amazon places a tracking cookie on your device to attribute any qualifying purchases per the Amazon Associates Operating Agreement.

5. European & UK Resident Rights (GDPR)

If you reside in the EEA or UK, you possess comprehensive statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access (Art. 15): Request confirmation and copies of personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten", Art. 17): Request deletion of your email address from our systems and MailerLite.
  • Right to Restriction (Art. 18): Request restriction of processing under certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): Receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Art. 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.

To exercise any of these rights, email WeCare@TheVibeCheckProject.com with the subject line "GDPR Data Request". We respond within thirty (30) days without charge.

6. California Resident Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents are entitled to specific disclosures:

  • Categories of Personal Information Collected: Identifiers (email address, IP address) and Internet/network activity data (via Clarity and AdSense cookies).
  • Business Purpose for Collection: Operating the Platform, delivering email affirmations, and serving display ads.
  • Notice of "Sale" or "Sharing": We do not sell your personal information for monetary payment. However, our use of Google AdSense may be classified as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under California law.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You may opt out of cross-context behavioral advertising through Google Ad Settings or the DAA opt-out tools linked in Section 3(D).
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never deny services, charge different prices, or provide a lower quality of experience because you exercised your CCPA rights.

7. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The Platform is strictly directed toward users aged 13 and older (or 16 and older in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or maintain personal information from children under 13.

If a parent or guardian discovers that their child under 13 has submitted their email address to our newsletter, please contact us immediately at WeCare@TheVibeCheckProject.com. We will verify your identity and immediately purge the minor's email from all mailing databases and records.

8. Data Retention, Security & International Transfers

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Email Addresses: Retained until you click "unsubscribe" or request manual deletion, after which your address is permanently purged.
  • Card Messages: Retained zero days on our servers. Messages exist ephemerally in URL parameters and on your local device.
  • Security: All traffic to and from the Platform is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • International Data Transfers: If you access the Platform from outside the United States, your information is processed in the United States and subject to United States jurisdiction. Third-party vendors comply with Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) where required.

9. Amendments to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological, legal, or operational updates. All revisions will be posted with an updated "Effective Date." Continued use of the Platform following notification constitutes acknowledgment of the revised policy.
